# Examples

Real sessions, kept as they were played. Open any save with the CLI
(`node cli.mjs look <file>`) or import it into the browser app.

- **play-session.json** — "The Last Ferry," a story session from
  version 1.0: the success oracle and checks carrying a short tale on
  their own.
- **play-session-1.2.json** — "The Golden Fleece," a story session from
  version 1.2 showing the wider toolkit: a character sheet, blessings,
  yes/no questions about the world, and how-many counts, with a readable
  log beside it (play-session-1.2-log.txt).
- **life-session.json** and **life-session-log.txt** — "The Seed Vault,"
  a life in the village of Alderhollow, Days 1–9, lived under LIFE.md.
  It was begun before the day counter existed, so its days are marked in
  the prose rather than by the counter; the log is the record exactly as
  it stands.
